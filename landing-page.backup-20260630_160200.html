1

Rental Car & Transportation Network

Platform Concept: Turn the Tesla rental website into a focused, self-service funnel for rideshare and delivery drivers — and make low-cost commercial insurance the hook that brings them in and keeps them. Design every element around driver intent: apply, rent, and insure directly online. No offline friction.

📋 See detailed concept brief: Phase_1_Rental_Insurance_Platform.md

  • Intent-Driven Design: Every page element earns its place—single purpose, clear conversion path
  • End-to-End Self-Service: Apply → Rent → Insure all online; minimize manual back-and-forth
  • Commercial Insurance Hook: Partner with insurers for low-cost coverage; earn margin per policy
  • Referral Engine: Drivers earn commissions for referrals; track and pay out automatically
  • Insurance Economics: Validate driver price vs. our margin per policy (pending validation)
  • Referral Mechanics: Commission structure, payout velocity, fraud prevention (pending validation)
  • Insurance Partners: Identify provider with platform integration capability (pending validation)
  • Tier 1: Rent-to-own (0-100k miles) with equity build
  • Tier 2: Turo public rentals (100k-130k miles) for secondary income
  • Tier 3: Hotel partnerships (Radisson, etc.) for daily rentals at $100+/day
