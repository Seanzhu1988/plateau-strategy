Walking guide

Working prototype. Walk, and it says what you are passing.

It only speaks when it is sure. A phone in a city street is often tens of metres out. When the fix is too rough to know which side of you a place is on, this says nothing rather than guess, a guide that says left when it means right is worse than a silent one.

Your location stays on this device. Places are looked up from OpenStreetMap by your browser; no coordinate is sent to our server.
