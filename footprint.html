Footprint recorder

Walk a corridor once, and the trace becomes the map.

This records a corridor, not a person. Pick the corridor, press Start at one end, walk it normally, press Stop at the other. What is kept is the line, its length, and roughly how long it takes, no name, no clock, no device.

Only corridors we have opened can be recorded, and only a signed-in owner can submit. If it refuses the walk, the reason says what to do, usually just walk it again; indoor GPS misses sometimes.

You are not signed in. Sean signs in at Dispatch; everyone else uses the username and password Sean issued them (the same sign-in as the shared pages), then comes back here. This page submits on your authority, and that authority is what makes a trace worth keeping.
