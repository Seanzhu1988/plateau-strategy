# Core tour destination-photo source audit

Verified: 2026-09-12. Scope: Freedom Trail (16), National Mall (21), Philadelphia (19), Mount Rushmore (8). Exact stop IDs, names and order come from `trails.json`.

## Result and publication boundary

64 of 64 stops have at least one destination-specific private-preview photograph. There are 75 photographs: Boston 16, National Mall 21, Philadelphia 28, Mount Rushmore 10. Four stops have an explicitly restricted candidate marked `previewOnly: true`, `needsRightsReview: true` and `rightsNote`: Korean War Veterans Memorial, Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial, LOVE Park, and the Lakota, Nakota and Dakota Heritage Village.

The first three have an identified photographer license or public-domain photograph, but modern artwork rights have not been cleared by this source audit. The Heritage Village image is supplied by the exact stop's official NPS page, but its photographer-credit field is blank. Do not treat an official host as blanket reuse permission, and do not strip these flags when merging. These four are usable private references, not publicly cleared additions.

No visitor photographs, original uploads, private submissions, secrets or personal records were accessed. No paid provider was called. No image files were downloaded, and nothing was pushed or deployed.

## Evidence and method

- Commons category listings and actual File pages identify candidates. Three low-volume `imageinfo` / `extmetadata` batches resolve canonical image URLs, photographer, license, source page, capture date and described subject. Parent-supplied Philadelphia 1 through 10 choices were checked again in the consolidated metadata batch.
- The Old South Meeting House color photograph lists its author as "myself" and source as own work. The upload metadata identifies Sokoljan, retained as the credit rather than leaving a meaningless "myself" label.
- Official NPS stop pages provide the exact Avenue of Flags, Grand View Terrace and modern Lincoln Borglum Visitor Center photographs, each explicitly credited "NPS Photo". Reuse status is grounded in [the NPS ownership statement](https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm), which distinguishes government-created work from third-party content and requests acknowledgement. A commercial copyright notice should not claim ownership of original U.S. Government works.
- Heritage Village uses [its own official destination page](https://www.nps.gov/places/youth-exploration-area-and-lakota-nakota-and-dakota-heritage-village-summer-only.htm), not another state's Heritage Village or a generic picture of Indigenous people. Its missing credit triggers a private-reference restriction.
- The evening ceremony uses the Commons photograph titled "Mount Rusmore lighting ceremony.jpg" (the original filename misspells Rushmore), captured on July 22, 2017. A separate, correctly described NPS night view is supplementary, not passed off as a photograph of the stage program.
- Source identity and license metadata were checked. This does not claim every photograph has been reviewed at full resolution or successfully loaded through the destination slideshow. The browser image tool could not open several Commons thumbnail URLs. No download workaround was used. The integrating agent should check actual delivered subjects, image load success, mobile framing and visible attribution before release.

## Wrong-subject choices excluded

- Boston Latin School: the historic School Street site is represented by the Franklin statue at Old City Hall, not today's school campus.
- Boston Massacre: use the actual location marker, not an engraving or newspaper clipping.
- Bunker Hill: omit the similarly named railroad photograph. USS Constitution: omit wood/copper fragments and photographs whose main ship is Coast Guard Eagle.
- National Gallery of Art: a building facade, not a Manet painting. Natural History: the museum exterior, not a specimen. African American History and Culture: its exterior, not a historical portrait card. Vietnam: the memorial wall, not a veteran portrait.
- National Museum of the American Indian: the National Mall building, not an interior object or the New York museum. Hirshhorn: its circular building, not only a sculpture garden. FDR: a real memorial waterfall, not the seed map.
- Rushmore's visitor center: the NPS photograph identifies the present Lincoln Borglum facility below the terrace, not the demolished earlier center or a generic entrance.

## Honest framing and rights limitations

These are dated photographs, not live conditions. Boston Common is a 2016 autumn view; King's Chapel 2009; Old South 2008; Old State 2006; Faneuil Hall 2005; Paul Revere House 2006; Old North 2011; Bunker Hill 2007. The USS Constitution view records an underway demonstration in 2014, not its present berth. Philadelphia City Hall is an aerial view. Franklin Court is the identifiable courtyard context for the compound museum stop. The Philadelphia Museum of Art image shows its main facade and does not promise a separate Rocky statue close-up. The Heritage Village image shows the covered structure and wooded approach, not year-round interpreters or a program. The Presidential Trail image is a documented view from that trail, not its entire walking route.

Photographer licenses do not automatically resolve modern sculpture rights, publicity rights or all jurisdiction-specific restrictions. Preserve author, source and license links. Any later crop or modification must be indicated and applicable share-alike conditions retained. Only the original photograph is sourced here; the site does not own it.

## Reproduction check

Read-only validation compares the JSON with `trails.json`: exact four trail IDs, 64 ordered numeric stop IDs and exact names; at least one image per stop; 75 total images; nonempty `src`, `alt`, `caption`, `credit`, `sourceUrl`, `license`; HTTPS image/source URLs; no image duplicated between different stops; no visitor-upload paths; all four rights-review entries retain both private flags and an explanation. This validates source data, not runtime slideshow delivery or legal clearance.

Only `audits/stop-photos-core.json` and this source audit were written for this task. All UI, tour order, audio, geometry, Gallery privacy and provider behavior remain outside this handoff.
