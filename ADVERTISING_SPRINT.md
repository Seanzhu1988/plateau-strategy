## ⚠️ Rules that protect the accounts

Standing checklist, violating any of these can cost an account, a vehicle, or a legal exposure that dwarfs the marketing upside. Re-read before every launch step.

1. **No paying client rides in the Tesla, ever, until the WUTC charter/excursion certificate exists.** Not "just to the viewpoint." Transporting passengers for compensation without the RCW 81.70 certificate is the exact violation the workaround exists to avoid. Sean drives solo; tours are walking-only from client meet-up onward.
2. **No public rental listing before the S10 compliance answer and the S11 insurer answer.** An impounded Tesla or an uncovered total-loss claim erases a year of rental revenue in one event.
3. **Never hand over the vehicle without the full agreement:** photo ID + license match, proof of approved TNC account, refundable deposit collected, off-app-use clause signed, Tesla-app monitoring disclosed and acknowledged. No exceptions for "he seems legit."
4. **Never publish an insurance claim on the site or in ads that the insurer hasn't confirmed.** Misstating coverage to a renter who ends up uncovered is a misrepresentation exposure, not a typo.
5. **No wire transfers, no CashApp, no payment before the in-person meet, in either direction.** This protects against scams AND is the ad's stated legitimacy signal; breaking it once breaks the pattern.
6. **GBP: answer "travels to meet customers," never edit multiple fields quickly, no PO box, video must match the profile exactly.** One clean shot; a suspension appeal costs weeks of the compounding asset.
7. **Craigslist: "cars+trucks by owner" section only**, the rideshare section is a carpool board and the ad gets flagged.
8. **FB groups: read and follow each group's posting rules before the first post.** A ban is permanent shelf-space loss; there are only a handful of relevant Seattle groups.
9. **No commission offers to front-line hotel staff.** Written partner-program proposal to management only; per-staff incentives only after the property authorizes in writing. A conduct complaint poisons a property permanently.
