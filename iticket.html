iTicket

One booth for the whole trip: the museum, the tour, the boat. Being built in the open by a licensed guide, which means this page will say what works, what does not, and what it is waiting on, rather than announcing a finished thing that is not finished.

What it is meant to be

A traveller plans a day here already: the trip planner lays out the walking, the destination book says what is worth the time, the museum maps get you to the room. The one thing missing at the end of that is the ticket itself, and today you leave to go and find it somewhere else.

iTicket is meant to close that gap, and to do it without becoming another wall of listings. The plan you already made is what chooses the tickets.

Where it actually stands

Honestly, and in order of difficulty. The hard part of ticketing is never the checkout. It is the supply.
