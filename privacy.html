Privacy

What this site collects, why, who else sees it, and how to make us stop. Last updated 9 August 2026.

We do not sell your personal information. Not to anyone, not in any form, not de-identified and not "shared for analytics".

There are no third-party analytics on this site and no advertising trackers. Visitor numbers are counted by our own server as daily totals.

We do not track your location. The visitor features that use your position — the "Use my location" button on the booking form and the walking-guide prototype — never send it to our server. In both, your browser asks OpenStreetMap directly: once to turn your position into an address, once to ask what is near you (see "Who else sees any of it"). The walking guide's trail of where you have been lives and dies on your phone. You can always type an address instead.

This is a small business, not a data business. The short version: we keep what is needed to drive you somewhere, to run driver and agent accounts, and to know roughly how many people visited. Everything below is detail on that.

What we collect

Booking a ride Your name, email, phone, pickup and drop-off address, the date and time, how many passengers, and anything you write in the notes. We need these to send a car to the right place.
A customer account Name, email, phone, and your password stored as a hash. An account is optional — you can book without one.
A driver account Name, email, phone, your vehicle's details including the VIN, and your date of birth, which is what you sign in with.
An agent account Name, email, phone, organization if you have one, and the agent code we issue you.
Signing a driver agreement Your typed name, the signature you draw, the date and time, your IP address and your browser's user-agent string. The last two are kept for one reason: they are the evidence that a particular person signed a particular version, which is what makes the signature worth anything.
Comments and ratings on places Whatever you type. No account is needed, so these are not connected to a name unless you put one in the text.
Simply visiting A daily count of page views per page, and a one-word label for where the visit came from — "google", "direct", "reddit". Never the full address of the page you came from.

How visitor counting works, exactly

Our server keeps one record per day: how many page views, which pages, the coarse source labels above, and how many bookings resulted. To count unique visitors it holds a random id for your browser for that day only. When the day closes, the ids are deleted and all that survives is a number.

That is a deliberate trade and it has a cost worth stating: because the ids are thrown away, we cannot go back and remove your device from a past day's number. Only today can be corrected. We think a system that cannot reconstruct who visited when is worth more than one that can be edited.

You can switch counting off for your device entirely, at /not-a-traveler. It sets a preference that stops both the visit count and the booking count for that browser.

Who else sees any of it

Only these, and only for the job named:

  • Square — card payments and invoices. They receive what a payment needs.
  • Twilio — the text messages that tell a driver about a job. They receive the message and the number it goes to.
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles, place search and the route tools. Your browser calls them directly, so OpenStreetMap receives your IP address and what you typed into a place search. It does not pass through our server, and we never see it. This is how every map on the site works, including the trip planner and the road-trip tool.
  • Wikipedia and Wikidata — descriptions of places, fetched by our server. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Google — only if you use a "Continue with Google" button. We receive your name and verified email address to fill the form in, and store neither beyond what you then submit. This feature is currently switched off.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to establish or defend a legal claim — for example, producing a signed driver agreement in a dispute.

Our own hosting provider (Render) necessarily handles the traffic to this site, as any host does.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not share it with anyone for their own purposes.
  • We do not run third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not target ads at you based on anything here.
  • We do not send your information to an AI system. The assistant on this site is available only to the owner when signed in, and visitor data is not put through it.
  • We do not record visitors' movements. The walking guide's trail stays on your device, and our server never receives your position from it or from the booking form. The only movement traces we keep are of our own team deliberately walking public corridors — an airport hallway, a station platform — to map them, recorded on our own sign-in, with no visitor and no name in them.

Washington consumer health data

Washington's My Health My Data Act requires a separate notice about consumer health data. Ours is short:

We collect no consumer health data. We do not ask about your health, we do not infer anything about it, and we do not collect precise location information — which the Act treats as health data when it could indicate that someone sought health services.

A pickup or drop-off address you type into a booking is used to drive you and for nothing else: it is not analysed, not categorised by what is at that address, and not used to infer anything about you. We do not collect, share or sell consumer health data, and there is accordingly no list of categories to disclose, no third parties to name, and nothing for which we would need your authorization.

If that ever changes, this section changes first, and we will ask for your consent before collecting anything of the kind — not after.

How long we keep things

  • Bookings — kept as business records.
  • Accounts — until you ask us to delete them.
  • Signed agreements — kept while they could still matter to a claim.
  • Daily visitor counts — the per-day totals are kept for about four months and then dropped. The browser ids behind the unique count are deleted at the end of each day, as above.

What you can ask for

  • A copy of what we hold about you.
  • Correction of anything wrong.
  • Deletion. We will delete what we are not required to keep, and tell you plainly what is left and why — a signed agreement, for instance, cannot be deleted while it is still operative.
  • To stop being counted, which needs no request at all: /not-a-traveler.

Ask by writing to the address below. We will not charge you, and we will not make you create an account to ask.

Depending on where you live you may have further rights under your local law, and nothing here is meant to reduce them.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. A child can of course be a passenger on a booking made by an adult; the booking is the adult's.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change means we would collect something new about you, we will ask first.
