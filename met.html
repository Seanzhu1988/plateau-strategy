Inside the Met, on footprints

The map on your phone goes grey the moment you step inside. This is a schematic of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, drawn by us, not to scale, with the rooms where they really sit. Tap the rooms you want in the order you want them, and footprints walk the route with honest times.

Times marked with a tilde are estimates until one of our surveyors walks that corridor with the recorder. A walked corridor shows its measured minutes. That is the footprint rule: a person who knows the way walks it once, and the walk becomes the map.
