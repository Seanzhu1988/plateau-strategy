Two blueprint systems

One is on the live site now. One is what we built together. They overlap, so here is each, plainly, before you choose.

A · Sealed blueprint under each idea

Live now

The pitcher's own sealed detail

What it isWhen someone pitches an idea, they can attach a sealed blueprint and a drawing, the working detail they do not want public.
To open itA reader signs in, and their name is recorded. Free, no payment.
MoneyNone.
BackingThe old buttons stayed: register to invest, or register to launch and run it.
The pointProtect the idea, and know exactly who has looked at the detail.

B · The Blueprint Deck, pay to view

What we built

The lab's blueprints, sold as a product

What it isA separate deck of the lab's own blueprints, each with a teaser anyone can read and a price you set.
To open itLog in and pay. A purchase, not an investment.
MoneyCard payment through Square, once it is connected.
BackingWe removed invest and launch. Backing a project now means buying its blueprint.
The pointLet people back a project by paying for the sheet, and fund the work.

Where they clash

The word "blueprint" means two different things. In A it is a pitcher's sealed detail. In B it is the lab's paid sheets. Same word, two objects.
Backing is the real fork. A keeps the free invest and launch buttons. B removes them and makes backing mean paying for the blueprint. You told me earlier you wanted B.

They can also live together

These are not truly enemies. A pitcher can still seal their own detail (A), while the lab sells its blueprints on the Deck (B). The only thing that cannot be both ways at once is the backing buttons: either the free invest and launch stay, or they are replaced by pay for the blueprint.

Nothing is pushed. Tell me which way the backing should go, and whether to keep the sealed-per-idea detail alongside the Deck, and I will make it one clean site and put it live.
